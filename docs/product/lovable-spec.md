# Project Overview

Build a modern, bilingual (English and Spanish) landing page for an AI-Assisted Risk-Based Quality Engineering service.

The objective is to generate qualified business conversations, not to showcase a traditional portfolio.

The landing should communicate professionalism, engineering expertise and trust.

---

# Objectives

The landing must:

- Explain the service in less than 10 seconds.
- Generate trust.
- Encourage visitors to start a conversation.
- Support English and Spanish.
- Be responsive.

---

# Target Users

Primary

- CTOs
- Engineering Managers
- QA Leads

Secondary

- Product Owners
- Recruiters

---

# Design Principles

The interface should feel like an engineering product.

Not a freelancer portfolio.

Not a marketing website.

Not a startup landing full of animations.

Prioritize:

- Clarity
- Simplicity
- Readability
- White space
- Professionalism

# Content Rules

Never generate placeholder text.

Do not invent services.

Do not invent customer testimonials.

Do not invent statistics.

Do not invent certifications.

Content must follow:

- copywriting.md
- offer.md
- value-proposition.md

---

# Constraints

Do not add extra pages.

Do not add pricing.

Do not add blog.

Do not add FAQ.

Do not add animations unless they improve usability.

Do not modify the information architecture.

---

# Definition of Done

The landing is complete when:

- All four sections exist.
- Navigation works.
- Language switcher works.
- Contact form is visible.
- Mobile experience is responsive.
- The page communicates the value proposition clearly.
