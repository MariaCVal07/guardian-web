# Overview

This document defines the identity of the brand.

It establishes the principles, personality and communication style that guide every interaction with customers.

The objective is to create a consistent and trustworthy brand focused on AI-Assisted Quality Engineering.

---

# Mission

Help software teams improve software quality through deeper analysis, risk-based thinking and Artificial Intelligence.

The goal is to enable better engineering decisions before software reaches production.

---

# Vision

Become a trusted reference in AI-Assisted Quality Engineering by helping organizations build more reliable software through intelligent quality analysis.

---

# Brand Principles

## Engineering First

Technology should support engineering, never replace it.

---

## Risk Before Defects

Every software defect begins as an unidentified risk.

Finding risks early creates better software.

---

## AI as an Assistant

Artificial Intelligence accelerates analysis.

Engineering judgment makes the final decision.

---

## Clarity Over Complexity

Complex ideas should be communicated in a simple way.

Customers should always understand what they receive.

---

## Evidence Over Marketing

Real examples create trust.

Claims without evidence do not.

---

# Brand Personality

The brand should be perceived as:

- Analytical
- Professional
- Curious
- Precise
- Trustworthy
- Practical

The brand should never feel:

- Arrogant
- Overly technical
- Sales-focused
- Sensationalist

---

# Brand Voice

The communication style should be:

Clear.

Professional.

Educational.

Direct.

Honest.

Every message should help customers understand software quality better.

Artificial Intelligence should never be presented as magic.

It should be presented as an engineering tool.

---

# Brand Values

The brand believes that:

Quality starts before testing.

Understanding the business is more important than executing tests.

Artificial Intelligence should augment engineers, not replace them.

Software quality is a business responsibility, not only a QA responsibility.

---

# Success Criteria

Every visitor should perceive the brand as:

A trusted engineering partner.

A specialist in AI-Assisted Quality Engineering.

A professional focused on improving software quality through better decisions rather than simply generating more test cases.
