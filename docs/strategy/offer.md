# Overview

This document defines the core service offered by the business.

The service combines Artificial Intelligence with Risk-Based Quality Engineering to improve software quality through deeper analysis, earlier risk identification and better testing decisions.

Artificial Intelligence is an accelerator.

Engineering judgment remains the foundation of every recommendation.

---

# Purpose

Help software teams identify quality risks before they become software defects.

The service supports engineering teams throughout the quality analysis process by combining structured QA methodology with AI-assisted analysis.

---

# Problem

Software projects often begin with:

- Ambiguous requirements
- Missing business rules
- Undefined acceptance criteria
- Hidden functional risks
- Limited testing coverage

These issues usually remain undetected until development or testing has already started, increasing cost and reducing product quality.

---

# Solution

The service performs a structured Quality Engineering review supported by Artificial Intelligence.

Instead of focusing only on executing tests, the service helps teams understand what should be tested, why it matters and where the greatest risks exist.

The objective is to improve software quality before execution begins.

---

# Service Workflow

Every engagement follows the same methodology.

1. Understand the business context.

2. Analyze functional requirements.

3. Validate business rules.

4. Identify functional risks.

5. Discover critical and edge scenarios.

6. Design a risk-based testing strategy.

7. Generate prioritized test cases.

8. Produce engineering recommendations.

Each phase is supported by Artificial Intelligence while maintaining engineering validation.

---

# Customer Deliverables

The customer receives a structured quality report that may include:

- Requirement Analysis
- Requirement Validation
- Business Rule Analysis
- Risk Assessment
- Critical Scenarios
- Edge Cases
- Risk-Based Testing Strategy
- Prioritized Test Cases
- Quality Recommendations

The exact deliverables may vary depending on the engagement.
