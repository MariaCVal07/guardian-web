# Overview

This document defines the primary audiences for the business.

The objective is to understand who experiences the problems we solve, how they make decisions, and how our communication should address their needs.

This document guides messaging, landing page content, marketing and future service evolution.

---

# Primary Persona

## QA Lead

### Role

Responsible for ensuring software quality across the team.

### Goals

- Improve testing quality.
- Increase testing coverage.
- Reduce escaped defects.
- Optimize QA processes.

### Challenges

- Limited time for requirement analysis.
- Manual creation of test scenarios.
- Incomplete business context.
- Pressure to release faster.

### What They Need

A faster way to analyze requirements while maintaining engineering quality.

---

# Secondary Persona

## Product Owner

### Role

Defines product requirements and business rules.

### Goals

- Deliver clear requirements.
- Reduce misunderstandings.
- Build features correctly the first time.

### Challenges

- Ambiguous user stories.
- Missing acceptance criteria.
- Constant requirement changes.

### What They Need

Early feedback before development begins.

---

# Decision Maker

## Engineering Manager / CTO

### Role

Responsible for software delivery and engineering quality.

### Goals

- Reduce production issues.
- Improve development efficiency.
- Increase confidence in releases.

### Challenges

- High cost of defects.
- Rework caused by unclear requirements.
- Difficulty scaling quality processes.

### What They Need

A repeatable quality process supported by Artificial Intelligence.

---

# Shared Pain Points

Across all personas, the most common problems are:

- Ambiguous requirements.
- Hidden functional risks.
- Time-consuming analysis.
- Incomplete testing coverage.
- Defects discovered too late.

---

# Shared Desired Outcomes

Every customer wants:

- Better software quality.
- Faster analysis.
- Greater confidence.
- Better engineering decisions.
- Reduced project risk.

---

# Communication Guidelines

When communicating with these audiences:

- Speak about business outcomes.
- Avoid unnecessary technical jargon.
- Demonstrate real examples.
- Explain the reasoning behind recommendations.
- Position AI as an assistant, not as a replacement for engineers.

---

# Success Criteria

Every piece of communication should make at least one of these personas think:

"This solves a problem my team faces today."
